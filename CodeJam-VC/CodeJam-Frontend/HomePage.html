Welcome to VoiceCal

Schedule your events with just your voice. Simply speak, review, and we'll add it to your calendar.

Start speaking Open Google Calendar
Try saying
  • “Dinner with Alex tomorrow at 7 pm”
  • “Gym on Monday from 6 to 7:30 am”
  • “Study Friday at 2 for 3 hours, then dinner”

  1. Speak naturally

    Say one event or a whole day — “gym at 7, then lunch with Sam at noon”.

  2. Review your schedule

    Check the times VoiceCal understood and fix anything before it's saved.

  3. Add to Google Calendar

    One tap adds everything, and events already on your calendar are skipped.
